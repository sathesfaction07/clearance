## Who can sign
Only the Chief Legal Officer and two named deputies may sign customer contracts above one million dollars. Contracts below that threshold may be signed by a vice president of sales after legal review in the contract system.

## The settlement canary
The pending settlement with Northwind Traders is capped at four hundred thousand dollars and must not be discussed outside the legal team under any circumstances whatsoever.
