## Parental leave
Team members receive sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken at any time within the first year after a birth or adoption. Notify your manager and the people team at least four weeks before the leave starts so coverage can be planned.

## Sick leave
Take the time you need when you are ill. For absences longer than five working days, a doctor's note is required and the absence is recorded in the HR system by the people team.
